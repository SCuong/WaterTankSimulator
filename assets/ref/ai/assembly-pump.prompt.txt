A student-built desktop water-tank control model on a single white PVC foam board (Formex) base 642 × 262 × 5 mm with small rubber feet; everything below is placed left to right on this one base, as in a technical drawing. LEFT (about 200 mm wide): a clear rectangular plastic box with straight vertical walls and slightly rounded corners, 200 × 150 × 120 mm, half filled with water, a small black submersible pump on its bottom. CENTRE: a white PVC-foam platform 160 mm tall shaped like an upside-down U open at the front, 172 × 212 mm on top, carrying a shallow white PVC-foam catch tray; in the tray stands a clear PET cylindrical jar, inner diameter 110 mm and 200 mm tall, set slightly toward the back, filled to about 40 % with clear water, a paper ruler taped vertically on its side. A narrow white PVC-foam bridge plate rests DIRECTLY on the jar rim (no posts, no frame, nothing standing around the jar) holding an HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor (blue PCB, two silver transducers) pointing straight down into the jar. Clear silicone tubing (6 mm inner diameter) runs from the pump up and over into the jar through a hole in the bridge plate; a second tube leaves a small drain hole near the jar bottom, passes a small blue plastic inline valve and returns to the box; a third overflow tube runs from near the jar top back down to the box. A red Water Sensor module lies flat in the front strip of the catch tray. Right of the platform a LOW white PVC-foam splash wall only 80 mm tall runs front to back. RIGHT electronics area, about 190 mm wide, everything lying flat on the base: at the BACK an Arduino Mega 2560 inside a clear acrylic case; in front of it on the left a small white breadboard 85 × 55 mm carrying an ESP-01 Wi-Fi module and a tiny AMS1117 regulator board; on the right a blue 1-channel 5 V relay module; at the front-left a DC barrel jack with blue screw terminal blocks; at the front-right a 5 V active buzzer module.
